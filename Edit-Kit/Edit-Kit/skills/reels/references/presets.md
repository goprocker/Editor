# Vertical presets

Each preset = palette + fonts + caption style + signature effects + pace. Theme colours from `/theme` override.

## Real Estate
Sub-looks:
- **Agent Story** (agent talking to camera): giant word behind the agent (HOPE, SOLD), big condensed numbers
  (1.7 MILLION, 60 DAYS), checklist chips for services, red X chip for "what others do", status card (MLS · waiting),
  target ring + buyer avatars, kinetic captions. Punch-in every sentence. Accent from the room (cobalt on warm wood,
  gold on cool/white), red for negatives. Fonts: Inter Tight 900 + Instrument Serif / Playfair Italic.
- **Luxury Walkthrough** (gimbal + drone): thin white single words mid-frame, giant word behind the agent or a building,
  white script accent words, a new shot every 1.5-2.5 s. Warm natural grade. Playfair Display + Inter Tight.
- **Project Launch Ad** (developer): location pin chip + "ACTUAL SHOT" tag top, wide extended gradient words,
  specs end card (beds, sq ft, storeys, phone, QR). Inter Tight + Cormorant/Playfair gold caps.

## Construction / Trades
Default palette: concrete #2b2b2b, off-white #f2f0ea, safety yellow #ffc400, steel blue #4a6a85.
Fonts: Anton (headlines, numbers), Inter Tight (captions), JetBrains Mono (measurements), Playfair Italic (one emotional word).
- **Project Progress**: speed-ramped timelapse, DAY 01 → DAY 90 counter, phase progress bar (Foundation, Frame,
  Roof, Finish), blueprint lines drawing on then dissolving into the real footage, stat cards (sq ft, days, crew).
- **Contractor Talk**: Bold Impact captions, measurement lines with dimensions snapping onto walls ("24 ft"),
  checklist ticks (permits, inspection, handover), "ACTUAL SITE" pin.
- **Renovation Before/After**: before/after wipe slider with a yellow handle, room labels, cost counter, giant
  reveal word behind the house (DONE, BUILT), hazard-stripe diagonal wipe transitions.

## Bold Impact (motivation, business tips)
Dark grade, yellow #ffd400 + red #e5262b. Montserrat 900 Italic all caps key words, 1-3 words per phrase, centred
~45-50% height. Zoom on every cut (1.0/1.12; hard cuts are part of this look, confirm the user wants them), emoji/icon pops every 2-3 s, black-and-white hit on punchlines,
phone mockup cards. A cut every 2-2.5 s.

## Ali Abdaal Clean (education, productivity)
Cool bright room, pastel cards (rose, teal, sage). Instrument Serif titles + Poppins key words, numbered list builds,
serif stat numbers, glass tip cards, soft blur transitions. A cut every 3-3.5 s. Calm SFX.

## Faceless Kinetic (no footage)
Black or paper background with a grid, one accent (violet #7b3ff2 or green #6cff2e). Kinetic headlines, floating UI
cards, list builds, full-frame colour card inserts, marquee text, colour flash transitions. A change every 1.2-2 s.
Voiceover via `/sound`.

## Podcast Clip
Near black, lime glow #d8ff3a on numbers. Exo 2 / Inter Tight italic captions (host CAPS, guest lowercase),
cut on the speaker (use the transcript, scene detection misses similar shots), speaker name tags, quote cards,
money counters. A cut every 1.5-3 s.

## Finance / Money
Dark Neon base (near-black, mint #3ef0b0 or blue). Price/stock graph draw-on with a leading dot, money counters,
% change chips (green up, red down), comparison bars, highlighter sweep on screenshots.
